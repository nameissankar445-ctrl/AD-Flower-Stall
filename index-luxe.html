Our Story

A family stall, rooted in the market

AD Flower Stall started as a small stall in the local flower market with one simple promise — only the freshest flowers, at an honest price. Today, families, temples and event planners trust us for every occasion.

We pick our flowers each morning, arrange them by hand and never keep stock that isn't fresh.

  • Bought fresh every morning, never stored for days
  • Hand-arranged by experienced florists

FAQ

Good to know

How do I place an order?

Add flowers to your order on this page and tap "Send on WhatsApp", or simply call us. We confirm quickly and arrange delivery or pickup.

Do you deliver?

Yes, same-day local delivery is available. Delivery charges depend on distance and are confirmed when you order.

Can you make custom or bulk arrangements?

Absolutely — weddings, temples, shops and events. Message us your date, colours and budget for a quote.

How fresh are the flowers?

We buy fresh every morning and only sell what we'd be proud to gift ourselves.
