Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What are your opening hours?

AD Flower Stall is open every day from 5:00 AM to 9:00 PM, including festival days. Flowers are picked and arranged before sunrise, so the freshest choice is available in the morning. You can also message the stall on WhatsApp at +91 99491 79933 to check availability before you visit.

Where is the stall?

AD Flower Stall is at Shop No: 4, Rythu Bazar, near NTR Bus Stand, Thamma Ranga Reddy Nagar, Guntur, Andhra Pradesh 522001. Search AD Flower Stall on Google Maps for directions, photos and reviews, or message us on WhatsApp at +91 99491 79933 if you need help finding the stall.

How do I order flowers?

Choose your flowers on the website, add them to your order and send it on WhatsApp at +91 99491 79933. We confirm availability and the final price there. You can pick up at the stall in Rythu Bazar or ask for same-day home delivery in Guntur.

Do you deliver in Guntur?

Yes, AD Flower Stall offers same-day delivery across Guntur. Send your order on WhatsApp at +91 99491 79933 with your delivery address, and we confirm the delivery charge and the final price there. Pickup from the stall at Rythu Bazar is also available.

Do you supply flowers for weddings and pujas?

Yes. AD Flower Stall supplies garlands and flowers for weddings, pujas, temples, house functions and festivals, including varmala garlands, bridal jasmine and stage flowers made to order. Send the date and what you need on WhatsApp at +91 99491 79933 and we reply with rates, free of charge.
